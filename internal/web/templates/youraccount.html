How passkeys work here

A passkey is a pair of keys: your device keeps the private one, and Roostack keeps only the public one, in its database (/var/lib/roostack/roostack.db, table passkeys). It's made for the lab's domain, and Roostack will accept it on its sign-in page and its own pages only. Your device checks it's you each time, with your fingerprint, face, or PIN, so a passkey is both steps of signing in. Through the SSH tunnel, or before the lab has a domain, browsers won't use passkeys, so your password and code stay.
