Each device has its own WireGuard key and address. It connects to {{.Server}} at {{.Endpoint}} (UDP), and Roostack's firewall and web proxy let it reach only what it's allowed to. Roostack makes each device's key when you add it, shows it to you once, and keeps only the public half.
