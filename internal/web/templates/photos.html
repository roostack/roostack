2Get your photos from Google

Google gives you everything in Google Photos as a few large zip files, called a Takeout.

This can be very slow, so it may take you several days. Google can take a day or more to make the files, and downloading hundreds of GB from Google is often slow and sometimes stops part-way. Take your time: Roostack keeps your place, so close this page whenever you like and come back {{if .Panel}}to Migrate photos on {{.Title}}'s page{{else}}to this page{{end}} when the files are ready.

  1. Open takeout.google.com{{template "new-window"}}, signed in to the Google account whose photos these are.
  2. Click Deselect all, then tick only Google Photos. Leave "All photo albums included" as it is.
  3. Click Next step, and choose: Send download link via email, Export once, file type .zip, size 50 GB. Then Create export.
  4. Google emails you when it's ready. Download every part (each is a zip of up to 50 GB) within 7 days: Google lets you download each one 5 times.
  5. Copy all the parts into one folder on a USB drive or your NAS. Don't unzip them: Roostack reads them as they are. You need about as much room as Google Photos says you use, plus a tenth.

Your Google account isn't changed: your photos stay in Google Photos until you delete them there. Tell me more{{template "new-window"}}
