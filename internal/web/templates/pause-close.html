Close public access completely

For a security problem in Roostack itself or in Caddy, which every visitor reaches first. Pausing shows visitors a page; closing won't let their connections in at all.

{{if .Closed}}

Public access is closed completely, since {{when .Closed.Since}}{{with .Closed.By}} by {{.}}{{else}} from the server's command line{{end}}.

{{if .IsOwner}}Open it again in Settings → Public access{{else}}An Owner can open it again from Settings → Public access.{{end}}

{{else}}
{{with .Advisory}}

The service registry warns of a security problem in Roostack {{.Version}} itself: {{clause .Title}} ({{.ID}}{{if .Exploited}}, being used in attacks{{end}}). {{if .Fixed}}Roostack {{.Fixed}} will fix it, and Roostack will open public access again by itself once it's updated.{{else}}There's no fixed release yet.{{end}}

{{end}}

What will happen

  • {{icon "globe"}}Within a few seconds, every server's firewall will refuse connections from the internet to your web addresses, whichever way they come in, so visitors will get a connection error rather than a page.{{if .Relay}} Roostack Cloud's relay will be asked to close its edges for your addresses too.{{end}}
  • {{icon "home"}}At home and on your lab's VPN, everything will keep working as it is.
  • {{icon "check"}}Nothing else will change: the services will keep running, and their addresses, settings, and certificates will stay as they are.
  • {{icon "pause"}}It will stay closed until an Owner opens it again{{if .Advisory}}, or Roostack is updated past the problem{{end}}, and Roostack will email the Owners now to say who closed it.
{{if .Internet}}

This server is on the internet, so Roostack's own pages will be closed too. You'll reach them only through your lab's VPN or an SSH tunnel. Before you close, check that one of them works: run this on your computer, then open http://127.0.0.1:8480.

ssh -L 8480:127.0.0.1:8480 you@{{.TunnelHost}}
{{end}}
Cancel
{{end}}

Good to know

For a problem in one of your services, pausing is enough, and Roostack's own pages stay open.

From the controller's command line, a technical user can close it with sudo roostack public pause -closed and open it again with sudo roostack public resume -closed.
