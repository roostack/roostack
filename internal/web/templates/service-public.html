How Roostack knows

Whenever its public addresses change, and once a day, the lab tells Roostack Cloud what serves each address that comes through its roost.ac name or the relay: the registry package's name and whether the registry flags it, or only that a package is your own, never its name. Roostack Cloud answers with those it doesn't allow, leaves them out of roost.ac's DNS and its relay, and Roostack keeps them private here.

If the check link doesn't open

Look at these in order: whatever is in the way is usually the first one that's wrong.

  1. Roostack's own firewall lets web traffic in (TCP 80 and 443, UDP 443). It always does.
  2. {{if .Internet}}Your provider's firewall allows those ports (above). Roostack can't see it from here. {{else if .Edge}}{{or .Edge.Name .Edge.ID}} is connected to the lab, and its provider's firewall allows TCP 80 and 443 (above). {{else}}Your router forwards those ports to this server (above).
