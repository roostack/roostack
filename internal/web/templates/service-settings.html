Updates

Updates are shown on its page and under “Needs attention”, and you apply them.

Fix releases go on automatically after the nightly backup. Larger updates are shown for you to apply.

Fix and feature releases go on automatically after the nightly backup; not new major versions, which can change how it works.

Every update goes on automatically after the nightly backup, new major versions too.

When automatic updates wait

Automatic updates need backups set up, and wait for you when an update has notes to read. If the service registry says the version {{.Service.Title}} runs is dangerous and Roostack stops it, a fix it would take automatically is applied straight away.

Saving restarts {{.Service.Title}} when its address, settings or libraries change; it's unavailable for a moment.
