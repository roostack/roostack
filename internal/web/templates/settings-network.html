It passes other names on to {{join . ", "}}.

It's the roostack-dns service on {{or .OwnServerName "the server running it"}}, and its log is journalctl -u roostack-dns. The firewall sends port 53 (UDP and TCP) from the home network and the lab's VPN to its port {{labDNSPort}}. It reads the names it answers from /var/lib/roostack/dns/names.json, and it answers AAAA and HTTPS questions for them with nothing.
