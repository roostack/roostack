What will happen

  • {{icon "globe"}}Within a few seconds, visitors from the internet will see a page saying it's closed for a while, whichever way they come in.
  • {{icon "home"}}At home and on your lab's VPN, everything will keep working as it is.
  • {{icon "check"}}Nothing else will change: the services will keep running, and their addresses, settings, and certificates will stay as they are.
  • {{icon "pause"}}It will stay paused until an Owner opens it again, and Roostack will email the Owners now to say who paused it.

Good to know

Roostack's own pages won't be paused, so you'll be able to open things again from here.

Away from home? This page works over your lab's VPN too. If Roostack's pages can't be reached at all, a technical user can pause from the server's command line: sudo roostack public pause -all
