Your backup plan

{{len .Entries}} {{if eq (len .Entries) 1}}backup{{else}}backups{{end}}

Keep 3 copies of your data, on 2 kinds of storage, with 1 away from home. Then a failed disk, a mistake, ransomware, or a fire or theft at home can't take everything.

Tell me more

This is the "3-2-1" rule, the usual advice for keeping data safe:

  • 3 copies. Your data on {{if .Server.This}}this server{{else}}{{.Server.Name}}{{end}} is the first; two backups are the others. If one copy is lost, you still have two, so there's time to replace it.
  • 2 kinds of storage. Copies on the same kind of storage can fail the same way. Disks count as one kind, whether in the server, plugged into it or in another server; online storage or a friend's lab is another kind.
  • 1 away from home. A fire, flood or burglary takes everything in the house, backups included. Online storage, a friend's lab, or a server somewhere else survives it.

Roostack backs up every night, to each backup below. Backups are encrypted, and readable with your recovery key. Restores use the main backup first: it's best at home, where restoring is quickest. Extra backups past the three are optional.
