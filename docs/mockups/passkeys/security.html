How passkeys work here

A passkey is a pair of keys: your device keeps the private one, and Roostack keeps only the public one, in its database (/var/lib/roostack/roostack.db, table passkeys). It's made for the lab's domain, lab.grahamhq.com, and Roostack will accept it at my.lab.grahamhq.com and roost.lab.grahamhq.com only. Your device checks it's you each time, with your fingerprint, face, or PIN, so a passkey is both steps of signing in. Through the SSH tunnel, or before the lab has a domain, browsers won't use passkeys, so your password and code stay.
