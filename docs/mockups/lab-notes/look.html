Name and look

Your lab's name, logo, and colours, wherever the lab shows itself: the sign-in page, the status page, and Roostack's own pages.

Name

Shown at the top of every page, and to other labs you share backup storage with (as "Smiths' lab's storage"). Roostack will restart to use a new name everywhere, which will take a few seconds.
