Turn it off for a while

For a service you don't need for now, or one you'd rather nobody used until you've looked at something.

Roostack will stop Photos and turn its address off, and it will stay off, after restarts and updates too, until you turn it on again here. Nothing will be deleted:

  • its photos, its database, and its settings will stay as they are;
  • its last backup will be kept, and backups won't take a new copy while nothing changes;
  • its address and port will stay reserved for it, so nothing else will take them.

Everyone using it, the family on their phones too, will get a page saying it's turned off.
