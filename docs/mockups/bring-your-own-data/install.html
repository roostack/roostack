Name and address

Its name for folders and backups: immich

Where it runs

Its data

Immich starts empty: you make its administrator below, then sign in and add photos.

From Docker Compose, another server or a backup of your own: its files and a dump of its database. Roostack installs Immich and leaves it stopped; the next page brings your data in, and Immich starts with it: accounts, albums and settings too.

Immich's settings

Its administrator and accounts come with its data, so Roostack doesn't make them.

What Immich sets up

Immich waits for its data. It's installed and stays stopped until you bring its data in, on the next page. Nothing is copied until you say so.
