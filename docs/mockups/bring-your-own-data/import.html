From your own copy of its files and a dump of its database: from Docker Compose, another server or a backup of your own. Roostack only reads your copy, and changes nothing until you switch.

Immich is running with your data. Switched today at 15:22, in 3 minutes.

Check it: sign in with your old account, open an album, search for something, and upload from a phone. Then look in Immich's Administration → Settings for anything that names the old machine, such as its external domain (used in share links). Moving Immich says what else to look at.

T7 Shield is unmounted: unplug it whenever you like. If something's wrong, you can bring the data in again, or remove Immich and start over.

6Switch to this data

6Switch to this data

First, on the old machine:

  1. Stop the old Immich, and keep it stopped: phones would carry on uploading to it. In its Compose folder: 
    docker compose stop immich-server immich-machine-learning
    And give both restart: "no" in its Compose file, so they don't start again with the machine.
  2. Take the dump again, now that nothing writes to the database, onto your copy beside library: 
    docker exec immich_postgres pg_dump --format=custom --no-owner --username=postgres immich > main.dump
  3. Bring your copy up to date, if you made it by hand: rsync copies only what changed. A share holding the original folders is up to date already.

Then Roostack:

  • Copies what changed since 14:52, once more.
  • Puts the copy in place of Immich's folders. Immich has no data yet, so nothing is set aside.
  • Loads main.dump into Immich's database.
  • Starts Immich, and waits for it to answer. If anything fails, Immich goes back to how it was.

Usually a few minutes; one piece of maintenance, so no backup or restart happens in the middle.

On the old machine

Roostack never runs anything there. What you do:

  1. Copy its folders onto a drive, or into a folder here with rsync, or leave them on your NAS and choose the share: Roostack only reads them.
  2. Take a dump of its database: 
    docker exec <your postgres container>immich_postgres pg_dump --format=custom --no-owner --username=<user>postgres <database>immich > main.dump
    Filled in from your old setup. Custom format only, for now: Immich's own nightly backups (.sql.gz) can't be loaded yet.
  3. Before the switch, stop it and keep it stopped: two Immichs would each think they're the one.
