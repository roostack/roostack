Into their own Immich account, with a key they make there, from their own page. You're emailed when one starts and ends.

Where they can migrate photos from

Additional locations

Folders you set up for people to migrate from: on a drive, on nuc, or on a share with your login. Everyone who can migrate photos sees what's in them, so a folder per person keeps them apart.
