2Get your photos from Google

2Get your photos from Google

Google gives you everything in Google Photos as a few large zip files, called a Takeout. It takes a few minutes of your time, then Google needs from a few hours to a few days to make it.

  1. Open takeout.google.com (opens in a new window), signed in to the Google account whose photos these are.
  2. Click Deselect all, then tick only Google Photos. Leave "All photo albums included" as it is.
  3. Click Next step, and choose: Send download link via email, Export once, file type .zip, size 50 GB. Then Create export.
  4. Google emails you when it's ready: usually the same day. Download every part (each is a zip of up to 50 GB) within 7 days: Google lets you download each one 5 times.
  5. Copy all the parts into one folder on a USB drive or your NAS. Don't unzip them: Roostack reads them as they are. You need about as much room as Google Photos says you use, plus a tenth.

Your Google account isn't changed: your photos stay in Google Photos until you delete them there. Tell me more

4Your Immich key

4Your Immich key

The photos go into the Immich account the key belongs to, so the person whose photos these are makes it, signed in to Immich as themselves. Roostack never needs an Immich password.

  1. Make a key in Immich (opens in a new window) Opens your Immich settings at API keys. Signed in as someone else there? Sign out of Immich first.
  2. New API key: name it Roostack import and tick these 15 permissions. Roostack says if any are missing.
    The permissions
    • asset.read
    • asset.statistics
    • asset.update
    • asset.upload
    • asset.copy
    • asset.delete
    • asset.download
    • album.create
    • album.read
    • albumAsset.create
    • server.about
    • stack.create
    • tag.asset
    • tag.create
    • user.read
  3. Create, then copy the key Immich shows you, and paste it here.
