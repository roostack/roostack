2 · Zipline → Public access (its address is kept private)

Who can reach shots.smiths.roost.ac

Kept private

You made this address public, but it's staying private: Roostack Cloud only allows public access for Zipline through roost.ac and its relay for approved accounts. Roostack Cloud will allow it once your account is approved: write to hello@roostack.cloud to ask.

The service registry's reason:

Anything uploaded to it can have a public link, and its short links can lead anywhere, so it's a favourite for hiding phishing.

Roostack will make it public by itself as soon as Roostack Cloud allows it, and until then only your home network (or VPN) can open it. Your own domain isn't limited, as long as visitors don't come through Roostack Cloud's relay: Your lab's address.

How Roostack knows

Whenever its public addresses change, and once a day, the lab tells Roostack Cloud what serves each address that comes through its roost.ac name or the relay: the registry package's name and whether the registry flags it, or only that a package is your own, never its name. Roostack Cloud answers with those it doesn't allow, leaves them out of roost.ac's DNS and its relay, and Roostack keeps them private here. Last answer: today at 09:14.
