Last change
Turned on by sam, 20 October at 14:02 · History

Let people sign in to Mealie with their Smiths' lab account, instead of a password of Mealie's own.

  • Roostack registers Mealie with the lab's sign-in page, and gives Mealie its settings and a secret.
  • Mealie restarts, which takes a few seconds.
  • Your own account in Mealie is found by its email (sam@smith.family), so you keep your recipes.
